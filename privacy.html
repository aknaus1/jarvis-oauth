Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

  • Who can use it: only the owner. The Google OAuth client is restricted to the owner's own account.
  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail (read, draft, send) and Google Calendar, only to act on the owner's requests.
  • Where data goes: data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Requests are processed by the owner's own AI model subscription.
  • Sending or changing anything (sending email, creating or editing events) requires the owner's explicit approval each time.
  • Revoking access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
